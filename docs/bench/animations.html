Clawd animation bench

The creatures on one clock, each on its own lattice (20 by 20 unless its caption says 40 or 60), drawn at the device's cell size (the 480 panel divided by the lattice: 24, 12 or 8 px) and at splash thumbnail size. Stock Clawd is the reference; the other two are proposals. Each cell prints its own lattice, frame count, palette and holds. Export gives claudepix-format JSON that tools/convert_to_c.js already reads.

  • Judge on the device size canvas; the small one only checks that the creature survives as a thumbnail.
  • Steam and the glitch are the loud states. The coffee cell spends 80% of its cycle in the quiet hold; decide on that, not on the sip.
  • Palette rule from the firmware: 10 colours max per animation, index 0 is transparent (drawn as panel black), cells are palette indices.
  • Lattice: 20 by 20 unless an animation sets size. 40 and 60 cut the 480 panel into whole 12 and 8 px cells; the 60 px thumbnail rounds each cell edge to a whole pixel, so a 40 cell thumbnail mixes 1 and 2 px cells.
