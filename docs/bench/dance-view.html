Dance view bench

The music card's dancing creature in the three dance views on the 480 by 480 panel, side by side on one clock: the same dancer, the same frame, the same card text in every cell, and the change of dancer (the 1.2 s shrink to a dot and back) at the same instant. Every position is the firmware's (ui.cpp, init_page_overlay and page_layout) and each cell prints its own. The pools and their order rules are the dance floor's: a feature for 90 s, then a clip for 30 s.

Redraw cost per dancer, settled

How to read this

  • Cost is measured, not timed. For every frame of a dancer the box of cells that differ from the frame before is what the firmware paints and hands LVGL to redraw (splash.cpp, mini_render); its area in panel px, summed over the dancer's loop and divided by the loop's length, is px redrawn a second. A frame's worth is 230,400 px (480 by 480). The dance floor average weights the features 3 to 1 against the clips, their 90 s and 30 s, each pool's members equally (the picks are random). The change of dancer is counted at LVGL's 33 ms refresh, each step redrawing the larger of the two scaled squares.
  • No browser time is shown on purpose: it would measure this PC, not the board. On the 4 inch board every redrawn px passes through PSRAM about four times (painted into the canvas, read, written to LVGL's draw buffer, copied into the framebuffer the RGB panel scans out), which is why the redraw count is the number that matters.
  • The dominant state is dancing: two changes of dancer of 1.2 s each per 122.4 s round, so the dancers dance 98 percent of the time. Decide on that column, not on the change.
  • Type is a stand in: the panel draws Tiempos 56 for the title and a mono 18 for the lines as bitmap fonts. Line boxes and positions match the firmware; glyph shapes do not. The album art is a stand in pattern, not a real cover.
  • The overlay is see through, never blurred. A blur would be a filter pass over every px the dancer redraws, on top of everything counted here, on an ESP32 whose RGB panel scans out of the same PSRAM; the strip's opacity (0 to 100, LVGL bg_opa of percent times 255 over 100) is the transparent option.
